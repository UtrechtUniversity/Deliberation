Welcome

Thank you for showing up on time and taking part in this study. Before we begin, here is a brief study overview. Please note that some tasks have a time limit

1. Reading task
You will read a short scenario and answer several questions about it.
2. Writing task
You will briefly explain your answers in your own words.
3. Waiting and group formation
After completing the tasks, you may need to wait while the other participants finish and the groups are formed.
4. Group task
Once the groups have been formed, you will continue to the next part of the study. Some groups will be assigned an additional discussion task.
Payment
You will receive £3.00 for completing the reading task, writing task, waiting phase, and the remaining standard parts of the study.
Participants assigned to the additional group discussion task will receive an additional £1.50.
In the unlikely event that we are unable to place you in a group, you will not be able to complete the remaining parts of the study. If you arrived on time but cannot participate further for this reason, you will still receive £1.50 for your time and effort.
