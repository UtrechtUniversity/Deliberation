Step 4

Discussion task

You have been grouped with three other players. Together, you will discuss the appointment decision described in the previous steps.

Share what you think about the decision and see where your views are similar or different.

Sometimes talking with others changes your mind. Sometimes it makes you more certain of what you already think.

Discussion guidelines
  • Treat the other participants respectfully.
  • Keep the conversation focused on the appointment decision.
  • Feel free to share personal experiences, but do not share identifying information.
