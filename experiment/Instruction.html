Step 4

Group discussion

You have been grouped with three other participants and will take part in a short chat discussion.

Goal

The goal of this discussion is to exchange perspectives, understand how others interpret the situation, and reflect on where you agree or differ. Engaging with different viewpoints may help you refine your own assessment.

Rules

To ensure a safe and constructive discussion for everyone, we ask all participants to abide by the following rules:

  • Express your views clearly and explain your reasoning.
  • Engage thoughtfully with others’ contributions.
  • Treat others with respect, even in disagreement.
  • Keep the discussion focused on the topic.
  • Do not share personal or identifying information.
  • Support equal participation of all group members.
