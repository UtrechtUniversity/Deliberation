Step 4

Committee discussion

You have been grouped with three other committee members. Together, you will discuss the hiring decision described in the previous steps.

Goal

The goal of this discussion is to exchange perspectives, understand how others interpret the situation, and reflect on where you agree or differ. As in many committee discussions, considering different perspectives may help you refine your own assessment.

Discussion guidelines

To ensure a safe and constructive discussion for everyone, please follow these guidelines:

  • Explain your reasoning clearly.
  • Engage thoughtfully with the perspectives of other committee members.
  • Treat others respectfully, even when you disagree.
  • Keep the discussion focused on the hiring decision.
  • Do not share personal or identifying information.
  • Help ensure that all committee members have an opportunity to contribute.
